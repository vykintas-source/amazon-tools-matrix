Methodology

Every cell in this matrix is verified from primary vendor sources or marked "Unknown". Pricing comes from official pricing pages where available, G2/Capterra/SoftwareAdvice where vendor pricing is private (clearly marked as possibly stale), or third-party reviews where no other source exists.

No cost projections for AMZ Bees at 30 brands are included in this document. Such estimates require quotes from Kapoq, Pacvue, Intentwise, Trellis, M19, CommerceIQ, Skai, and Stackline before any total stack cost can be calculated.

MCP Integration Verification

Five tools have shipped MCP servers as of May 22, 2026:

Sources

Primary vendor websites, G2 product pages, Capterra, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice, vendor pricing pages, vendor press releases, Amazon Ads developer documentation. Third-party blog content from atom11, SellerApp, SellerMate, AdLabs, and SellerLabs used where they contain factual product comparisons; their competitive positioning is treated with appropriate skepticism.
